## Project Overview

This project was developed as part of the **AnalystLab Africa Data Analytics Internship Programme – Week 2 Business Analytics Case Study**.

The project focuses on analysing the **SuperStore Sales Dataset** using Microsoft Power BI to provide management with an interactive view of sales performance, profitability, customer segments, product performance and regional trends.

The objective is to transform business data into meaningful insights that support data-driven decision-making.

## Business Problem

Management requires a business intelligence solution that provides a clear view of:

- Overall sales performance
- Profitability
- Regional performance
- Customer segment contribution
- Product and category performance
- Sales trends over time

The Power BI dashboard was developed to address these areas and provide actionable business insights.

---

## Business Questions

The analysis addresses the following business questions:

1. What is the overall sales performance of the company?
2. Which regions generate the highest sales and profit?
3. Which customer segments contribute the most revenue?
4. Which product categories perform best?
5. Which products are the most profitable?
6. What trends can be observed over time?
7. What recommendations should management implement to improve business performance?

## Key Business Insights

### 1. Consumer Segment Leads Revenue

The Consumer segment generated approximately **$1.16M**, representing about **50.56% of total sales**, making it the largest revenue-contributing segment.

### 2. West is the Strongest Region

The West region recorded the highest sales and regional profit, making it the strongest-performing region.

### 3. Technology Leads Category Sales

Technology generated approximately **$0.84M in sales**, making it the strongest-performing product category.

### 4. Sales Increased Over Time

Sales increased from approximately **$0.48M in 2014** to **$0.74M in 2017**, indicating positive overall sales momentum.

### 5. Profitability Requires Attention

The business generated approximately **$2.30M in sales** but only **$0.29M in profit**, resulting in a **12.47% profit margin**.

---

## Business Risks

### 1. Profit Margin Pressure

The 12.47% profit margin indicates that a relatively small share of revenue is converted into profit.

### 2. Consumer Segment Dependence

Consumer customers account for approximately 50.56% of sales, creating concentration risk if demand from this segment declines.

### 3. Regional Performance Imbalance

The West and East outperform Central and South, indicating uneven regional performance.

---

## Business Opportunities

### 1. Expand Consumer Revenue

Strengthen loyalty programmes and targeted promotions to increase retention and spending among Consumer customers.

### 2. Develop Central and South Regions

Investigate the causes of lower regional performance and introduce targeted marketing and sales strategies.

### 3. Capitalise on Technology Demand

Increase focus on high-performing Technology products through inventory planning and targeted promotion.

---

## Recommendations

1. **Improve profit margins** by reviewing pricing, discounts, product profitability and operating costs.
2. **Strengthen Consumer retention** through targeted loyalty programmes and personalised promotions.
3. **Improve Central and South performance** through region-specific campaigns and further investigation of sales barriers.
4. **Invest in profitable products** by prioritising high-performing products in inventory and promotional planning.
5. **Replicate West-region success** by identifying successful strategies and evaluating their application to lower-performing regions.
